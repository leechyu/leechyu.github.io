Effective date: September 1, 2026

This policy describes how the LedFrey iOS application ("the App") handles information. The short version: your ledger data never leaves your device.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

LedFrey is a fully local ledger app. We do not operate any server, and the App has no account system. We do not collect, transmit, or store:

  • Your ledger content — entries, amounts, notes, categories, or any accounting data
  • Ledger names, passwords, or recovery codes (these are stored only on your device, and protected ledgers are encrypted on disk)
  • Personal information such as your name, email, or phone number
  • Any analytics or crash reports about how you use the App

All ledger data exists only as files on your device. Deleting the App deletes all of its data. Backups are created by you (exported files) and remain under your control.

2. Third-Party Advertising

The App is free and supported by advertising. It includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob), which may collect the following data for advertising purposes:

  • Device identifiers (including IDFA, only if you grant permission via the App Tracking Transparency prompt) — used for ads
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address) — used for ads
  • Usage data (product interaction) — used for analytics and ads
  • Diagnostics (crash data, performance data) — used for analytics

This data is processed by Google under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. For users in the European Economic Area and the UK, a Google consent form is shown before personalized advertising is enabled.

You can decline tracking when prompted (the App works fully without it), and you can manage ad personalization at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device, or via Google Ads Settings.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

  • Ledger files (plain or encrypted) and the library index — stored in the App's own container
  • Encryption keys — stored in the iOS Keychain; Face ID / passcode entries never leave the secure enclave
  • App preferences (language, appearance, auto-lock timeout) — stored locally via system settings storage

None of this data is accessible to us or transmitted anywhere.

4. Permissions

  • Face ID (LocalAuthentication): used only to unlock your own password-protected ledgers on this device. Face ID data is never available to the App itself.
  • Tracking (App Tracking Transparency): optional; refusing it only affects ad personalization.

The App requests no other permissions (no contacts, photos, location services, camera, or microphone).

5. Children

The App is rated 4+ and is not directed at children. It contains no user-generated content sharing and no social features.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the App changes (for example, if advertising configuration changes). The latest version will always be available at this page, with the effective date shown above.
